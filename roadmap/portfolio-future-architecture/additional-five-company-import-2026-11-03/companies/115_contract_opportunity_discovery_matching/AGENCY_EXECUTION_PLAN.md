**Purpose:** Register and make this company BUILDABLE in the existing Command Center project system—not merely a roadmap file. The actual Work authorization appears in `04_WORK_HANDOFF_REGISTER_AND_ENABLE.md`; preparing this pack does not mutate anything.

**Company-specific commercial goal:** One industry-specific weekly qualified contract-opportunity briefing with source links, eligibility, due dates, amendment tracking, buyer matching and explicit NO-GO explanations.

**Protected actions:** Do not bid, sign, submit a proposal, certify vendor status, claim set-aside eligibility, create official registration, scrape gated systems, contact contracting officers, spend on opportunity access, or impersonate procurement agencies. API keys and data-provider terms require user action/authorization. The service sells intelligence, not government endorsement.

**Potential revenue:** Owner-approved flat monthly/quarterly company monitoring, scoped sector packs, verified data exports, premium deadline monitoring, API or white-label feed, optional permitted professional review; no bid awards or customer revenue guaranteed.
