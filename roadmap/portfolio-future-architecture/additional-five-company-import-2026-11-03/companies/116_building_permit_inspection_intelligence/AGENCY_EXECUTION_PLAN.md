**Purpose:** Register and make this company BUILDABLE in the existing Command Center project system—not merely a roadmap file. The actual Work authorization appears in `04_WORK_HANDOFF_REGISTER_AND_ENABLE.md`; preparing this pack does not mutate anything.

**Company-specific commercial goal:** Permit Application Readiness Pack for one project type in one jurisdiction: verified checklist, application/link inventory, missing information report, official fees/inspection references, and owner-review checklist.

**Protected actions:** No stamping design, structural calculations, legal interpretations, acting as licensed architect/engineer, representing municipal approval, guarantee, booking municipal inspection, filing forms, paying fees, or signing owner/contractor certifications absent role verification and action-specific authorization. **Potential revenue:** Fixed-fee per project permit-readiness packet; recurring contractor tracking tier, jurisdiction pack subscriptions, inspection readiness coordination, legitimate white-label tooling for permit expediters. Not legal/engineering work or permit approval guarantees.
