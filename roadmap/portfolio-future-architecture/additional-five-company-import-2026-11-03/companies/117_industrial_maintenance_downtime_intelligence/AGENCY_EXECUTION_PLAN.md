# Company 117 — Agency Execution & Build Plan

**Purpose:** Register and make this company BUILDABLE in the existing Command Center project system—not merely a roadmap file. The actual Work authorization appears in `04_WORK_HANDOFF_REGISTER_AND_ENABLE.md`; preparing this pack does not mutate anything.

**Company-specific commercial goal:** Fixed-scope Maintenance Backlog & Downtime Audit on a customer-provided CSV export: asset list health, repeated failures, overdue preventive maintenance, spare-parts gaps and a top-10 owner-reviewed action queue.

**Protected actions:** No direct PLC/SCADA/OT commands, equipment startup/shutdown, remote control, safety-system bypass, LOTO instruction replacement, calibration or repair instructions beyond manufacturer-approved documentation reviewed by qualified personnel. No claim of predictive maintenance from inadequate data. Private operational data must remain isolated. No paid sensors, gateway devices, site visits or integrations without explicit approval.

**Potential revenue:** Paid one-time maintenance data audit; per-site monthly reliability reporting; per-asset monitoring tiers; white-label data quality service for industrial maintenance providers; enterprise private deployment under owner-approved costs.
