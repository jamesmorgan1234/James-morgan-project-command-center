**Purpose:** Register and make this company BUILDABLE in the existing Command Center project system—not merely a roadmap file. The actual Work authorization appears in `04_WORK_HANDOFF_REGISTER_AND_ENABLE.md`; preparing this pack does not mutate anything.

**Company-specific commercial goal:** Synthetic and public-data-backed Opportunity Dossier: one product/SKU and channel with verified costs, demand, competition, supplier eligibility, compliance, capital requirement, and abstention when unknowable. Real sales only after owner approval and account compliance.

**Protected actions:** No automatic supplier purchase, inventory financing, marketplace posting, seller account onboarding, expense, advertising, credit, payout change, real order or refund without action-specific owner approval and certified account/marketplace authority. **Potential revenue:** Verified merchandise sale contribution profit after supplier landed cost, marketplace fees, shipping, returns, taxes and settlement; optional future commerce intelligence software ONLY through a separate approval and isolation review.
