## Read this first

This is an immediate, **best-available archive**, **not an official account export** and **not a mechanically exact copy of every assistant response**. No ChatGPT conversation-export API or full structured chat-dump was accessible in this session. To avoid silently fabricating a verbatim record, the archive separates:

1. **Original media**: `MEDIA/6238_original_uploaded_video.mp4` (byte-for-byte copy of the accessible original upload), original-derived contact sheet and ten keyframes.
2. **Conversation reconstruction**: `TRANSCRIPT/chat_chronology.md` and `TRANSCRIPT/user_messages.md`: user requests as preserved in visible conversational context, and accurately labelled summaries of assistant responses. The original extensive architecture specification is represented by normalized design documents rather than claimed verbatim reproduction.
3. **Decision ledger**: approved rules, $0-first policy, no-loss exposure, assignment-first model, authorization boundary, Command Center dependency check.
4. **Implementation-ready planning assets**: blueprint, specialists, local schemas, data provenance, math, workflow, roadmap, failure cases, and tests.
5. **Reference sources**: URLs discussed in the chat, included as **historical references**, not a claim they were reverified during this packaging step.

**No implementation/build of the real-estate project was authorized or performed.** Packaging this archive does not grant that authority. The Command Center dependency safety prompt remains the primary project boundary. No repository, deployment, seller, buyer, financial account, or production system was touched for this archive.

## Critical clarification

The project is at **planning/specification** stage. Real-world $0 upfront and no-loss outcomes are **targets and screening/authorization rules**, not literal guarantees. A $5,000 gross spread does not equal cleared bank profit.
