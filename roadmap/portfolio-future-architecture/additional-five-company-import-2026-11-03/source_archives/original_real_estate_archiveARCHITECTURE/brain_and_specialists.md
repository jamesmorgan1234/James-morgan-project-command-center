# Local brain — subordinate hierarchy

Command Center (permanent global top control plane)
  -> Real-Estate Acquisition Brain (project local)
      -> Specialist workers and deterministic guards

The brain never becomes a peer/superior to Command Center. Its connection adapter is an empty project-side seam until Command Center finalizes PIF.1–PIF.10. It may NOT define shared identity, memory, learning promotion, messaging, authentication, global governance, enterprise security or shared registries.

## 22 local specialists and boundaries

1. Acquisition Orchestrator — sequences eligible local deal tasks; no spending, signing or cross-project governance.
2. Source Intelligence — official data sources, source provenance, parsers, freshness, drift detection.
3. Property Identity — canonical parcel/address/owner linkage, dedupe, ownership contradictions.
4. Ownership & Equity — recorded instruments, taxes, estimated equity, no claim of clear title.
5. Distress & Motivation — objective property signals and seller-stated needs without exploiting vulnerabilities.
6. Valuation — as-is, ARV, liquidation, investor value ranges, comps and model disagreement.
7. Repair Intelligence — range estimates, condition signals, uncertainty and inspection needs.
8. Buyer-Market Intelligence — actual cash-buyer criteria, bids, liquidity, reliability and proof of funds.
9. Deal Economics — normal/conservative/stress spreads, days to cash, effort, close probability.
10. No-Loss Guardian — vetoes unapproved or excessive personal capital risk.
11. Compliance Brain — state law, disclosures, licensing questions, channels, outreach rules, professional review.
12. Seller Workflow — respectful communications, seller intent, offer preparation, follow-up state.
13. Contract Intelligence — assignability, documents, signatures, deadlines, approval and template control.
14. Title-Risk Intelligence — preliminary record conflict, curability and professional title dependency.
15. Buyer Matching — match qualified buyers, keep backups, use actual bid history.
16. Closing Intelligence — track verified title/funds/documents/closing and payout.
17. Fraud & Identity Guardian — spoofing, payment instruction changes, title/seller/buyer identity anomalies.
18. Adversarial Deal Reviewer — independently searches for disqualifying evidence.
19. Quality/Evaluation Brain — model calibration and reproducible test/forecast errors.
20. Outcome Learning — project-local, versioned learning candidates, no shared promotion.
21. Recovery & State Guardian — durable state, idempotency, crash and parser recovery.
22. Command Center Adapter — **placeholder only; no final shared contracts, actions or protocols**.

## Authority ladder
Global: Command Center is superior to the whole project. Locally: binding law and project constitutional constraints; James's required protected-action approval; independent compliance/no-loss veto; project orchestrator; specialists; automations. No AI competence score overrides human authorization or hard legal/compliance gates. The project must not create a global authority framework.

## Required local outputs
- Every recommendation exposes evidence ids, age, confidence, conservative value and risk level.
- Specialists can return PASS, FAIL, APPROVAL_REQUIRED, MANUAL_REVIEW, UNKNOWN or HOLD as relevant.
- Disagreement reduces certainty or prompts independent verification; it is not averaged into invented confidence.
- Learning requires a versioned evidence-backed evaluation and must never auto-weaken hard policy.
