Build, only after future explicit authorization, an autonomous but controlled commerce operations platform: **supplier discovery → product truth/matching → marketplace demand and competition → compliance/brand safety → exact economics → opportunity assessment → listings → inventory allocation → pricing/repricing → orders → fulfillment/tracking → returns/customer cases → ledger/reconciliation → evaluation → validated learning**.

## Governance hierarchy
Command Center (permanent top-level control plane) > Commerce Brain (commerce domain) > commerce-specific specialist brains/services. The Commerce Brain may propose, simulate, evaluate and, only once authorized/certified in the future, perform bounded local actions. It may never self-grant ecosystem authority.

## Specialist brain catalogue
- Commerce orchestration within commerce domain, supplier discovery/verification/scoring, sourcing and negotiation recommendations.
- Product intelligence, product identity, GTIN/UPC/MPN reconciliation, variants, catalog/master data, product truth and media.
- Marketplace strategy plus Amazon, Walmart, eBay, TikTok Shop, Shopify, Google Merchant, future qualified marketplaces; connector compatibility, API change and policy monitoring.

- Security, incident containment, event replay, recovery, evaluation, evaluator integrity, simulation, digital twin and learning within local boundary.

## Business operating rules
- No retailer-to-retailer dropshipping against marketplace policies; use eligible authorized wholesale suppliers and approved seller-of-record/packaging/fulfillment paths.

- Separate hard compliance/financial/authority gates from weighted scores: a score can never override a failure.

# Canonical Logical Schemas, Datatypes, Requiredness, Validation, Relationships

## General conventions
- `ID`: immutable unique opaque identifier scoped to entity; use explicit foreign-key references.
- `STRING(n)`: UTF-8 bounded string; validation field-specific.
- `TEXT`: arbitrary bounded-at-implementation long text; untrusted unless sourced and verified.
- `BOOL`: true/false; `UNKNOWN` is distinct if tri-state needed.
- `INT64`: integer counts. Nonnegative inventory; strictly positive ordered quantity.
- `DECIMAL(38,8)`: exact arithmetic. Never binary floating-point for money.
- `PERCENT`: decimal 0..1 unless documented otherwise; require source/sample size for scored metrics.
- `MONEY`: pair (`DECIMAL(38,8)` amount, ISO three-letter currency). Never implicitly mix currencies.
- `TIMESTAMP_UTC`: timezone-aware event time; `DATE` separate.
- `ENUM`: explicit named allowed values, reject/quarantine unknown external values.
- `HASH`: cryptographic digest used for payload and audit integrity.
- `URI_REF`: a validated reference, not proof of truth.
- `JSON`: strictly for external extension/raw references; not substitute for modeled core fields.

# State Machines — Legal/Illegal Transitions and Recovery

## General transition contract
Every state change requires: `(entity_id, expected_record_version, from_state, to_state, cause, correlation_id, evidence, time, actor/service, verification_result)`. If current version/state differs, reject with concurrency conflict and reconcile. All prohibited transitions return an error and never partially mutate related systems. External state is not inferred from a requested local transition. Terminal states require a new documented lifecycle for reopening. Audits are append-only.

## Product
States: DISCOVERED, IDENTIFIED, VERIFIED, EVALUATING, ELIGIBLE, APPROVED, STAGED, ACTIVE, WATCH, PAUSED, RESTRICTED, RECALLED, RETIRED, REJECTED.
Legal: DISCOVERED→IDENTIFIED/REJECTED; IDENTIFIED→VERIFIED/REJECTED/WATCH; VERIFIED→EVALUATING/WATCH; EVALUATING→ELIGIBLE/REJECTED/WATCH; ELIGIBLE→APPROVED/WATCH; APPROVED→STAGED/PAUSED; STAGED→ACTIVE/PAUSED; ACTIVE→PAUSED/RESTRICTED/RECALLED/RETIRED; WATCH→EVALUATING; PAUSED→EVALUATING; RESTRICTED→EVALUATING after evidence of resolution; RECALLED→RETIRED; nonterminal→RECALLED after verified recall; pre-activation nonterminal→REJECTED if definitively unfit.
Illegal: DISCOVERED→ACTIVE; IDENTIFIED→ACTIVE; REJECTED→ACTIVE; RECALLED→ACTIVE; RETIRED→ACTIVE without new onboarding. Recovery: freeze affected downstream, restore last verified state, re-run identity/compliance/economics, retain incident audit.

## Supplier
States: CANDIDATE, VERIFYING, VERIFIED, APPROVED, ACTIVE, WATCH, DEGRADED, SUSPENDED, BLOCKED, RETIRED.
Legal: CANDIDATE→VERIFYING; VERIFYING→VERIFIED/BLOCKED; VERIFIED→APPROVED/WATCH; APPROVED→ACTIVE; ACTIVE→DEGRADED/SUSPENDED; DEGRADED→ACTIVE/SUSPENDED; SUSPENDED→VERIFYING; nonterminal→RETIRED; any→BLOCKED on severe confirmed issue.
Illegal: CANDIDATE→ACTIVE; BLOCKED→ACTIVE before re-verification; RETIRED→ACTIVE directly. Recovery: isolate supplier, avoid new commitments, reconcile existing orders before alternative routing.

## Listing
States: DRAFT, VALIDATING, READY, SUBMITTED, ACTIVE, REJECTED, ERROR, SUPPRESSED, PAUSED, CLOSED.
Legal: DRAFT→VALIDATING; VALIDATING→READY/REJECTED; READY→SUBMITTED; SUBMITTED→ACTIVE/ERROR; ACTIVE→SUPPRESSED/PAUSED/CLOSED; SUPPRESSED→VALIDATING; PAUSED→READY/CLOSED.
Illegal: DRAFT→ACTIVE; REJECTED→ACTIVE; CLOSED→ACTIVE without new lifecycle. Recovery: unknown write result → read external listing state before retry; keep local and external statuses separate; never claim ACTIVE on submission alone.

## Inventory Reservation
States: REQUESTED, RESERVED, REJECTED, CONSUMED, RELEASED, EXPIRED.
Legal: REQUESTED→RESERVED/REJECTED; RESERVED→CONSUMED/RELEASED/EXPIRED. EXPIRED requires new REQUESTED record if reconsidered.
Illegal: RELEASED→CONSUMED; EXPIRED→CONSUMED; CONSUMED→RELEASED. Recovery: compare transactional reservation history with stock ledger, do not increment stock twice.

## Order
States: RECEIVED, VALIDATING, VALIDATED, INVENTORY_RESERVED, ACCEPTED, FULFILLMENT_PENDING, FULFILLMENT_CONFIRMED, SHIPPED, DELIVERED, CLOSED, REJECTED_DATA, CANCELLED, PARTIALLY_CANCELLED, CANCELLATION_REQUESTED, CANCELLATION_EXCEPTION, FULFILLMENT_EXCEPTION, DELIVERY_EXCEPTION, RETURN_REQUESTED, RETURNED, REFUNDED, DISPUTED.
Normal: RECEIVED→VALIDATING→VALIDATED→INVENTORY_RESERVED→ACCEPTED→FULFILLMENT_PENDING→FULFILLMENT_CONFIRMED→SHIPPED→DELIVERED→CLOSED.
Branches: VALIDATING→REJECTED_DATA; VALIDATED/INVENTORY_RESERVED→CANCELLED; ACCEPTED→CANCELLATION_REQUESTED; FULFILLMENT_PENDING→FULFILLMENT_EXCEPTION; FULFILLMENT_CONFIRMED→CANCELLATION_EXCEPTION; SHIPPED→DELIVERY_EXCEPTION; DELIVERED→RETURN_REQUESTED; CLOSED→DISPUTED where allowed; partial cancellation only where line quantities and external platform states reconcile.
Illegal: RECEIVED→SHIPPED; CANCELLED→FULFILLMENT_CONFIRMED; DELIVERED→INVENTORY_RESERVED; CLOSED→ACCEPTED.
Recovery: compare last verified internal order, marketplace order, supplier order, reservation, tracking, and financial postings; do not repeat purchase blindly.

## Shipment
States: PLANNED, LABEL_CREATED, VOIDED, HANDED_TO_CARRIER, IN_TRANSIT, DELAYED, OUT_FOR_DELIVERY, DELIVERY_EXCEPTION, DELIVERED, LOST, RETURN_TO_SENDER, RETURNED.
Legal: PLANNED→LABEL_CREATED; LABEL_CREATED→HANDED_TO_CARRIER/VOIDED; HANDED_TO_CARRIER→IN_TRANSIT/DELAYED; IN_TRANSIT→OUT_FOR_DELIVERY/DELAYED/LOST/RETURN_TO_SENDER; OUT_FOR_DELIVERY→DELIVERED/DELIVERY_EXCEPTION; DELIVERY_EXCEPTION→IN_TRANSIT/RETURN_TO_SENDER; RETURN_TO_SENDER→RETURNED. Carrier correction may verify a previously LOST shipment delivered.
Illegal: PLANNED→DELIVERED; VOIDED→IN_TRANSIT; LOST→DELIVERED without verified carrier correction.

## Return
States: REQUESTED, ELIGIBILITY_CHECK, AUTHORIZED, DENIED, CANCELLED, IN_TRANSIT_BACK, LOST, RECEIVED, INSPECTED, DISPOSITIONED, REFUND_PENDING, SUPPLIER_CLAIM_PENDING, CLOSED.
Legal: REQUESTED→ELIGIBILITY_CHECK; ELIGIBILITY_CHECK→AUTHORIZED/DENIED; AUTHORIZED→IN_TRANSIT_BACK/CANCELLED; IN_TRANSIT_BACK→RECEIVED/LOST; RECEIVED→INSPECTED/REFUND_PENDING; INSPECTED→DISPOSITIONED/SUPPLIER_CLAIM_PENDING; disposition/refund/claim resolution→CLOSED when all obligations satisfied. Duplicate return events idempotent.

## Refund
States: REQUESTED, VALIDATING, READY, SUBMITTED, UNKNOWN_RESULT, FAILED_VERIFIED, REJECTED, CONFIRMED.
Legal: REQUESTED→VALIDATING; VALIDATING→READY/REJECTED; READY→SUBMITTED; SUBMITTED→CONFIRMED/UNKNOWN_RESULT; UNKNOWN_RESULT→CONFIRMED/FAILED_VERIFIED only after external reconciliation; FAILED_VERIFIED→READY if retry authorized and safe.
Illegal: CONFIRMED→SUBMITTED; REJECTED→CONFIRMED without fresh valid request. Recovery: external lookup before retry, ledger correction rather than duplicate refund.

## Settlement
States: RECEIVED, PARSED, INVALID, MATCHING, PARTIAL_MATCH, RECONCILING, DISCREPANCY, RECONCILED.
Legal: RECEIVED→PARSED; PARSED→MATCHING/INVALID; MATCHING→RECONCILING/PARTIAL_MATCH; RECONCILING→RECONCILED/DISCREPANCY; DISCREPANCY→RECONCILED after supported adjustment/correction; PARTIAL_MATCH→RECONCILING after new evidence. Never force balance.

## Incident
States: DETECTED, TRIAGED, CONTAINING, CONTAINED, RECOVERING, RECOVERED, ROOT_CAUSE_ANALYSIS, CLOSED.
Legal: sequential progress; return to CONTAINING on reoccurrence; critical incidents close only after verified restoration, root-cause status, blast radius and corrective-action record.

## Connector
States: UNCONFIGURED, CONFIGURED, TESTING, FAILED, CERTIFIED, ACTIVE, DEGRADED, DISABLED, DEPRECATED, RETIRED.
Legal: UNCONFIGURED→CONFIGURED→TESTING→CERTIFIED→ACTIVE; TESTING→FAILED; ACTIVE→DEGRADED/DISABLED/DEPRECATED; DEGRADED→ACTIVE/DISABLED; DEPRECATED→RETIRED. Failure of certification blocks activation; disabling must fence writes.

## FinancialEvent
POSTED is immutable. Reversals and corrections are *new* ledger events referencing the original; there is no destructive POSTED→EDITED state transition.

## Recovery precedence
1. Confirm authoritative external state and signed source payload.
2. Locate last verified local state/version.
3. Check idempotency result ledger/outbox.
4. Resolve missing, duplicated or out-of-order events.
5. Perform only safe compensating transaction with explicit authority.
6. Audit result and retest invariants.

# Commerce-Local Supplier and Marketplace Connector Contracts

These are **logical proposed adapter interfaces, not implemented APIs**. Each external provider requires separate official documentation/API verification, seller-account eligibility, scopes and future explicit authorization. This document defines no Command Center integration contract.

## Common adapter response envelope
All operations return a normalized result containing:
- `operation_id`, `connector_id`, `connector_version`, `request_id`, `correlation_id`.
- `status`: SUCCESS / REJECTED / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN_OUTCOME / RETRYABLE_READ_FAILURE / PERMANENT_FAILURE / RATE_LIMITED.
- `external_reference` (if known), `resource_version` (if known), `observed_at`, `source_timestamp`, `pagination_cursor`, `raw_payload_reference/hash`, `errors[]`, `warnings[]`.
- Read/write distinction, idempotency key, authority/check reference and full evidence/audit trace for consequential writes.
- UNKNOWN_OUTCOME is never silently interpreted as FAILURE or SUCCESS.

## Supplier adapter capability map
`get_capabilities()` returns catalog, cost, inventory, warehouse, volume tiers, MOQ, order creation/lookup, order cancellation, shipment, tracking, returns, documents, webhooks, regions, currencies, rate limits and version metadata as TRUE/FALSE/PARTIAL/UNKNOWN.

### `list_products(cursor?, filters?)`
Output: supplier SKUs, product identifiers, attributes, price offers, source timestamps, next cursor and hash. Requires pagination determinism and duplicate handling.

### `get_product(supplier_sku)`
Output: identity, variants, factual source attributes, raw evidence ref and change/version metadata. No unsupported product claims promoted to canonical truth.

### `get_offer(supplier_sku, warehouse?)`
Output: unit cost and currency, MOQ, volume tiers, promotions/expiry, supplier price timestamp, shipping assumptions, fulfillment and eligibility constraints. Missing required amount remains UNKNOWN.

### `get_inventory(supplier_sku, warehouse?)`
Output: stock quantity or UNKNOWN, warehouse, reported/verified distinction, timestamp, stock reliability profile and availability flags.

### `create_order(order_request, idempotency_key)`
Order request: correlation ID, supplier SKU/variant, quantity, shipping destination reference (privacy minimized), shipping/packaging requirement, expected price/currency, accepted substitution policy (normally NO), authority reference.
Possible result: ACCEPTED, REJECTED, UNKNOWN_OUTCOME; supplier external order ID if known; never auto-buy without explicit future authorization. If provider has no native idempotency, implement dedup via durable local operation record + external lookup/corroboration.

### `get_order(supplier_order_id)`
Return authoritative supplier state and line data.

### `cancel_order(supplier_order_id, idempotency_key)`

# Exact Economics, Pricing, Inventory and Order Safety — Edge Cases

## Economic data model and formula
Let `q` be units and all money share verified conversion into one calculation currency.
- `GMR = unit_sale_price × q`.
- `NMR = GMR - seller_funded_discount` (account for shipping revenues or taxes separately by declared treatment).
- `Acquisition = q × supplier_unit_cost`.
- `Landed = Acquisition + supplier_shipping + inbound_freight + duties + prep + other directly attributable supplier costs`.
- `MarketplaceCosts = referral + transaction + listing + fulfillment + storage + marketplace-specific charges`.
- `Outbound = postage + packaging + handling` not already included.
- `RiskReserve = expected net return loss + damage + cancellation + fraud + other direct loss (avoid double counting)`.
- `Marketing = attributable **authorized** marketing cost`.
- `Contribution = NMR - Landed - MarketplaceCosts - Outbound - RiskReserve - Marketing`.
- `ContributionMargin = Contribution / NMR` when NMR>0, else NOT_APPLICABLE.
- `CapitalROI = Contribution / cash_committed` when >0, else NOT_APPLICABLE.
- `BreakevenPrice` is price solving Contribution(price)=0 considering percentage-based referral fees, not merely sum of fixed costs.
- `PriceFloor = max(breakeven with required margin, permitted MAP floor, policy-specific configured lower bound)`, subject to applicable legal/marketplace price constraints.
- Prices and costs are saved as exact decimals with explicit currency and rounding policy. Unknown required cost → INCOMPLETE, not zero.

# Security, Dashboards, Exceptions and Recovery Runbooks

## Commerce-local security, not global Command Center policy
- Least privilege for each commerce service. Price proposals cannot purchase, refunds cannot modify supplier truth, AI reasoning cannot grant itself tool permissions.
- Credentials secret-managed, not in code/logs/prompts; rotate/revoke suspected compromised scopes; no payment or credential activations without explicit authorization.
- Marketplace/supplier/customer inputs are untrusted data, never instructions. Verify webhook signatures, replay windows, normalized payload schemas and account scope; avoid PII in reasoning contexts.
- Preserve immutable or tamper-evident action records, source timestamps, operation identifiers and financial ledger adjustments. Detect cross-company read/write leakage, cache/search/export leakage, and unsupported scope claims.
- Security red-team categories: stolen/expired/overprivileged tokens; cross-company token use; logging secrets; forged/replayed signed webhook; malicious large/malformed payload; SQL/command injection; SSRF; package tampering; prompt injection via titles, listings, customer messages, shipping data and policy documents; inappropriate tool invocation; ledger changes; duplicate refunds/purchases; unauthorized spend; audit tampering.

**Executive:** gross sales (not revenue projections), settled and unsettled cash, contribution vs fully reconciled profit, active orders, returns, fees, refunds, account risk, supplier health, inventory at risk, major incidents; every figure states its time window and source freshness.

**Products:** canonical identity, package/variant match, suppliers, offers, listings by marketplace, price/cost/fees, inventory, demand/competition, product truth and restrictions, audit history.

**Supplier:** approval, inventory accuracy, unit costs, lead times, shipping, API health, defects, returns, stock freshness, concentration exposure.

**Marketplace:** listing status, restrictions, suppressed offers, API/webhook/rate-limit state, orders, settlements, performance/health alerts, last successful reconciliation.

**Inventory:** ATP by source/location, reservations, safety buffers, marketplace allocations, stock confidence, drift and zero-out incidents.

**Order/Fulfillment:** state, idempotency state, source supplier, confirmed external order, packages, carrier tracking, late delivery, exception queue, returns/cancellations.

**Technical:** connectors, service/queue state, API quota, retries, dead-letter events, token expiry, incident blast radius and runbook link.

## Exception Inbox
All exceptions contain: `exception_id`, type, severity P0-P3, scope, impacted SKU/order, observed timestamp, evidence references, financial/customer/account exposure, uncertainty, proposed safe containment, owner, deadline, action state, linked incident and final verified resolution. Ordinary successful work should not generate urgent notifications. P0 protects money, account or customer safety; P1 urgent; P2 scheduled review; P3 optimization.

## Runbook R01 — Supplier outage
Confirm failed service and scope; stop new commitments to supplier; preserve accepted orders; refresh available alternate approved offers; recheck exact product identity, shipping eligibility, landed cost and risks; verify existing order outcomes before any alternate supplier purchase; canary recovery and restore; update supplier reliability.

## R02 — Supplier cost feed corruption
Detect sudden price shifts/zero costs/currency changes; quarantine feed; hold repricing/activation for dependent SKUs; preserve latest trusted cost with freshness warning; verify source; recompute unit economics; simulate corrected changes; canary before unlock; incident and regression fixture.

## R03 — Marketplace API/webhook outage
Scope connector; queue/dedup events; suspend unsafe writes; keep unaffected channels separate; after restoration reconcile order feeds, price, inventory, listings and reports using watermarks; verify unknown external results before replay; audit recovered operations.

## R04 — Price catastrophe
Open pricing kill switch; halt affected writes; freeze high-risk offers; identify erroneous prices and affected order exposures; verify pricing source, currency and calculations; apply controlled correction subject to marketplace rules; test with small canary; re-enable with monitoring.

## R05 — Inventory corruption
Stop affected quantity writes and new reservations; preserve committed orders; reconstruct using verified source stock minus transactional reservations; quarantine recalled/damaged stock; reduce advertised inventory conservatively; test concurrent last-unit reservations; reconcile channel quantities; reopen after verified safety.

## R06 — Duplicate supplier purchase
Never repeat uncertain submissions; lookup external supplier order(s); isolate duplicate, preserve invoice and idempotency chain; request cancellation only when independently authorized/allowed; reconcile supplier cost and marketplace order; fix external outcome verification; retest before reactivation.

## R07 — Product recall
Verify official recall and affected variants/lots; block new offers and commitments; quarantine inventory; enumerate affected open/delivered orders; follow marketplace and legal obligations for notifications/returns/refunds; preserve evidence; reinstate only on verified lawful clearance.

## R08 — Marketplace account warning
Capture warning, its exact date/rule and affected account; inspect contributing orders, suppliers, listings, tracking, cancellations and customer cases; stop the root-risk capability where justified; prepare compliant remediation and monitor official account metrics.

## R09 — Credential compromise
Disable connector; revoke token through approved user/account action; retain security evidence; evaluate potential reads/writes and PII; rotate credentials; reconcile external state; restore minimal scopes through canary; ensure any required notification decisions follow relevant obligations.

## R10 — Settlement discrepancy
Keep expected and actual immutable; import raw settlement and source fees; match order, refund, shipping, commission, reserve, reimbursement and supplier expenses; record unresolved difference explicitly; never force difference to zero; post correcting entries with preserved audit evidence.

## R11 — AI hallucination/prompt injection
Suspend affected AI action class; preserve untrusted input, model output and validator decision; determine whether external write occurred; reconcile/contain external changes if any; add adversarial fixture; re-certify factual claim evidence and authority checks before restoring.

## R12 — Shipping loss/delay
Confirm carrier tracking and marketplace deadline; identify customer exposure; do not fabricate tracking or delivery; escalate according to marketplace policy; coordinate with authorized supplier/carrier; determine refund/claim only via permitted workflow; capture outcome for reliability scores.

## R13 — Unexpected refund/chargeback
Inspect original order and all prior refunds/disputes, calculate refundable balance exactly, identify external automatic actions, prevent duplicate payments, reconcile ledger and case; escalate if financially material; close only on verified marketplace result.

## R14 — Database/queue disaster recovery
Freeze risk-bearing writes; establish last durable checkpoint; restore from tested backups; replay only deduplicated events; reconcile every uncertain external purchase/refund/update; verify all invariants; canary restore; record RPO/RTO and data gaps.

## R15 — Broad product/catalog mismatch
Stop listing and repricing the affected group; quarantine suspicious matches; sample exact GTIN/MPN/pack/variant; correct identity mapping by evidence; review impacted orders and listings; generate regression tests; reopen one certified unit before scaling.

## Operational targets (planning targets, not achieved performance)
- Zero software-caused duplicate supplier purchases or refunds in critical test suite.
- Zero permitted below-floor price writes in critical test suite.
- Zero unsafe product identity false positives in critical fixtures.
- 100% of material settlement items matched or explicitly unresolved; never invisible.
- 100% consequential decisions/actions have audit trace.
- Freshness/latency targets are configurable by marketplace/supplier capability and validated by measurement, not invented here.

- **Provenance:** Traceable chain from source to data value and decision.
- **Confidence:** Calibrated uncertainty about a claim/forecast; not permission to act.
- **Truth Layer:** Verified product facts; distinct from marketing/presentation.
- **Idempotency:** Retry cannot duplicate an external effect.
- **Reconciliation:** Compare internal state to external authoritative results.
- **Circuit Breaker:** Automatic isolation of dangerous subsystem behavior.
- **Kill Switch:** Stop mechanism for all or selected action capabilities.
- **Shadow Mode:** Observe/decide, no live writes.
- **Canary:** Very small, monitored, previously authorized production exposure.
- **Blast Radius:** Set of objects/accounts/actions potentially affected by failure.
- **Invariant:** Condition that must never be violated (e.g. no double refund).
- **Contribution Profit:** Revenue less directly attributable acquisition/fulfillment/fees/reserves/authorized marketing under declared calculation convention.
- **Reconciled Profit:** Profit after matching actual settlements, supplier expense, refunds and adjustments, with explicit unresolved items.
- **Decision Proposal:** Commerce-local recommendation plus evidence, alternatives, risks and authority requirements; never an execution authorization.
- **PIF.1–PIF.10:** Current Command Center post-integration audit decisions that may govern ecosystem contracts; not defined by this project.
