## Business identity

Purpose: Turn customers’ owned or licensed long-form footage into reviewable, accessible, platform-ready short video deliverables with strong quality, traceable rights, and local-first processing.

Core problem: Podcasters, educators and businesses have hours of recorded video but limited time and budget to locate valuable highlights, edit vertical clips, add readable captions and deliver consistent high-quality content.

Value proposition: Reliable source-to-clip traceability, genuinely useful topic/context preservation, strong quality and caption review, platform-specific packaging, and private local-first processing rather than generic volume output.

Ideal first customer: Podcasters, educators and small business content teams with lawful recorded footage and regular needs for accessible short-form clips.

Monetization model hypothesis: Customer video-hour or clip-bundle fees, optional recurring production plans, optional premium human QC and brand kits; do not depend on paying for compute/stock/music APIs until explicit owner authorization.

Non-goal and protected boundary: Separate from personal-only VeloClip and creator/clipper campaign platform CrowdSkein. New business makes and delivers edited files; no automatic third-party channel uploads or creator payout/referral schemes by default.
