## Evaluation matrix
Every Brain must be evaluated on all five axes: (1) domain result accuracy against curated examples; (2) correct abstention/uncertainty on unsupported claims; (3) evidence/provenance grounding and current source versions; (4) privacy/role/owner authorization; (5) failure behavior including source outage, duplicate inputs and no-spend.
Domain eval examples:
- Low-resolution licensed images
- Fonts without commercial embedding rights
- Unlicensed cover art
- Complex math/RTL scripts unsupported
- Footnote crosslink corruption
- Widows/orphans and page-break bugs
- Cookbook images or recipes unverified
- Wrong bleed for print interior
- Wrong hardcover vs paperback cover geometry
- Missing original manuscript
- Source comment leakage
- Hidden track changes included in print
- ISBN mismatch across formats
- Customer requests silent substantive rewrites
- KDP changes trim/page constraints
- EPUB navigation broken on reader

Escalation and protected actions:
- Never publish or change customer KDP/publishing accounts without separate explicit authorization; default is delivery-only
- No unauthorized substantive edit to manuscripts; changes tracked and author approved
- No claiming manuscript rights, exclusivity, plagiarism clearance or copyright legal advice
- Never reuse customer manuscripts or covers for another client or training without explicit consent
- Screen sample art/license and no prohibited piracy or counterfeits
- Verify retailer specs on delivery date; platform accepts/rejects independently
- Keep client billing and all outgoing money protected behind owner approval
- Protect unpublished manuscripts with private local storage, access logs and retention rules
