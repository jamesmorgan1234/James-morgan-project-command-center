## Business identity

Purpose: Turn an author’s completed, lawfully provided manuscript into polished, validated, accessible, edition-specific publishing deliverables without taking over the author’s account, rights or publishing decisions.

Core problem: Independent authors face print-layout rejection, ebook navigation/accessibility errors, cover/spine sizing, mismatched edition metadata, inconsistent typography and rework when preparing files across distribution platforms.

Value proposition: Author-controlled, style-preserving conversion with traceable layout checks, real print/ebook QA, preflight evidence, and consistent multi-format releases at an affordable fixed scope.

Ideal first customer: Independent authors and micro-publishers who own manuscripts but want professional files without learning production tools.

Monetization model hypothesis: Per title and format packages, series/volume discount, revision allowances, expedited turnaround only if resources allow, optional recurring imprint catalog maintenance with no hidden paid provider dependencies.

Non-goal and protected boundary: Not a vanity publisher, literary agent, copyright lawyer, plagiarism-certification agency, ghostwriting commitment, book distributor, or autonomous KDP uploader. All final content, rights, cover, metadata, pricing, publishing and platform acceptance decisions stay with customers.
