## Business identity

Purpose: track ownership, rights, licenses, permitted/prohibited uses, territories, windows, exclusivity, sublicensing, usage, revenue allocation, royalty obligations, recoupment, statements, reconciliation, underpayment and audit for intellectual property and digital content.

Core problem: rights and royalty operations are fragmented across contracts, spreadsheets, collecting societies, labels/publishers, distributors, platforms, agencies, content systems and accounting. Ownership may be fractional and layered; permissions vary by territory, medium, term and exclusivity; usage arrives in inconsistent formats; deductions/recoupment complicate calculations; rights expire, conflict, transfer and fragment over time.

Value proposition: a versioned rights graph and royalty operating layer that connects contracts, ownership, permissions, usage and money with evidence, explains every allocation, alerts on conflicts/expiry/underpayment and supports clearance/licensing workflows without pretending to replace legal review.

Non-goal: not autonomous legal counsel; not a system that independently grants rights, represents disputed ownership as settled, pays royalties, signs licenses, waives rights or asserts infringement without authorized human/legal process.
