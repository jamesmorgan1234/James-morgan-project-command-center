# Company 016 — Regulation Change Monitor

**Company ID:** `company:future:016`  
**Primary project:** `project:company:future:016`

**Truth state:** PLANNED  
**Authorization:** roadmap/architecture only; no legal advice, regulatory filing or live compliance action.

## Business identity

Purpose: continuously monitor official regulatory/legal sources, detect changes, extract dates/requirements and map likely operational impact to subscribed industries/jurisdictions.

Problem: regulations, guidance, forms and deadlines change across many authorities; organizations learn late or rely on expensive manual monitoring.

Value: source-backed change timelines, requirement extraction, deadline alerts and workflow-impact mapping while preserving legal-review boundaries.

Delivery: subscription dashboard/alerts, API/feed, jurisdiction/industry packs, enterprise watchlists, private knowledge-base integration, OEM/embedded.

## Product tree

Official Source Registry; Legislative/Rulemaking Monitor; Regulatory Agency Update Monitor; Guidance/FAQ/Form Monitor; Deadline/Effective-Date Tracker; Requirement/Obligation Extractor; Change Diff & Timeline; Industry/Jurisdiction Packs; Policy/Procedure Impact Mapper; Compliance Task Generator (proposal only); Regulatory Calendar; Evidence/Archive; Executive Briefs; API/Feed; Enterprise Watchlists; Auditor/Legal Collaboration Workspace; Historical Regulatory Archive.

## Feature/customer/vertical tree

Features: source authority rank, jurisdiction, effective/proposal/comment dates, supersession, document version, diff, citation/page/section, requirement taxonomy, affected entity/product/process, confidence, legal-review state, alerts, calendars, saved topics, team notes, audit/export, accessible/localized UI.

Customers: compliance/legal ops, security/privacy, finance, HR, healthcare admin, manufacturers, SaaS/data/AI vendors, public contractors, consultants, associations, insurers, enterprises with multi-jurisdiction exposure.

Verticals: privacy/data, AI regulation, employment, tax/accounting rules, finance operations, cybersecurity, accessibility, healthcare administration, environmental, procurement/government contracting, ecommerce/consumer, licensing/credentialing. High-stakes interpretation always flagged for counsel/specialists.

## Brain capability tree / architecture

Brain understands source hierarchy, legal text structure, definitions, dates, obligations and business-process mappings. It summarizes and proposes impact/risk, but distinguishes enacted/proposed/guidance, verified text vs interpretation, and cannot replace legal advice.

Architecture: authoritative-source registry, scheduled/web monitoring, document parser/OCR, temporal/version store, citation graph, ontology, search/RAG, rules/date extraction, LLM for semantic comparison/requirement extraction with citations, human review status.

## Agent tree

Official Source Monitor, Document Diff Agent, Requirement Extractor, Deadline Agent, Jurisdiction Mapper, Industry Impact Analyst, Policy/Workflow Mapper, Citation/Fact Checker, Alert Curator, Legal-Review Coordinator, Archive Agent, Integration Agent.

## Deterministic services

Source fetch/scheduler; snapshot/hash; document parser; version/diff; date/calendar; citation store; taxonomy; alert rules; search/index; archive; evidence export; subscription routing; retry/rate limiting.

## Workflow

Register official source/topic → capture/version → detect change → classify legal status → extract citations/dates/requirements → map likely affected domain → confidence/review → alert with source → propose tasks/policy review → archive supersession/effective history.

## Integrations / API / data

Government/regulator sites/APIs/feeds, legislative sources, document stores, compliance/GRC, policy systems, calendars, task systems, email/chat, data warehouses. API for sources/documents/changes/requirements/deadlines/timelines/evidence.

Data moat: source registry, long version history, requirement ontology, jurisdiction/industry mappings, citation quality and reviewed impact mappings.

## Monetization / distribution

Jurisdiction/industry/topic packs, seats, entities, alerts, API/feed, historical depth, enterprise, OEM, custom monitoring. Distribution via compliance/legal tech partners, consultants/associations, security/privacy ecosystems and developer integrations.

## Security/legal/privacy

Use official/public/licensed sources. Preserve citations and source timestamps. No unsourced legal conclusions. Customer business-process mappings private. Regional copyright/licensing and legal-practice boundaries respected.

## Failure / edge cases

Unofficial mirror, draft vs final confusion, rescinded rule, emergency order, delayed effective date, source redesign/outage, PDF scan error, cross-reference amendment, jurisdiction ambiguity, conflicting guidance, court decision changing interpretation. Use authority ranking, explicit legal-status state, version graph, confidence and human review.
