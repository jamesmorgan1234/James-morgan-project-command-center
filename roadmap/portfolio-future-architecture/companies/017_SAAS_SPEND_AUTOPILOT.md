# Company 017 — SaaS Spend Autopilot

**Company ID:** `company:future:017`

**Authorization:** roadmap/architecture only; no cancellations, contract acceptance, purchasing or payments.

## Business identity

Purpose: discover software spend, identify waste/duplicate tools/unused seats, forecast renewals and recommend or execute preauthorized optimization workflows.

Problem: businesses accumulate overlapping SaaS, inactive licenses, surprise renewals, shadow IT and inconsistent pricing.

Value: visibility, benchmarked utilization, renewal calendar, consolidation and savings evidence.

Delivery: SaaS dashboard, finance/procurement integrations, browser/email/SSO discovery, API, enterprise private deployment, managed optimization.

## Product tree

Software Inventory; Spend Normalization; Seat/License Utilization; Duplicate/Overlap Detection; Shadow IT Discovery; Renewal Calendar; Contract/Price Benchmarking; Cancellation/Downgrade Planner; Consolidation Recommender; Procurement Request Crossover; SaaS Security/Access Review Crossover; Budget Forecast; Savings Tracker; Vendor Negotiation Intelligence (recommendation only); Department Chargeback; Employee Offboarding Seat Recovery; API/OEM; Managed SaaS Optimization.

## Feature/customer/vertical tree

Features: vendor normalization, transaction/invoice/contract matching, seat counts, active/inactive use, SSO/app access, owners/departments, renewal/notice terms, price/discount history, overlap taxonomy, security/compliance flags, savings confidence, cancellation steps, approvals, evidence, forecast, audit, accessible dashboards.

Customers: startups/SMBs, midmarket/enterprise finance/procurement/IT, agencies, distributed teams, MSPs, PE operating teams where lawful, franchises/multi-entity groups.

Verticals are broad; packs for SaaS-heavy tech, agencies, professional services, ecommerce, healthcare admin, education/nonprofit, multi-location businesses. Regulated environments add vendor/security/compliance checks.

## Brain / architecture

Brain resolves vendors/products, understands contract terms/usage and recommends keep/downgrade/consolidate/cancel/renegotiate. It estimates savings and risk but cannot cancel or buy without authorization. Deterministic contract/renewal/seat rules, financial transaction matching, SSO usage, vendor taxonomy, forecast engine; LLM for contract extraction and overlap explanation with cited source.

## Agents

Spend Discovery Agent, Vendor Normalizer, Utilization Analyst, Contract/Renewal Agent, Duplicate Tool Analyst, Shadow IT Agent, Savings Analyst, Consolidation Planner, Procurement Analyst, Security/Vendor-Risk Liaison, Offboarding Recovery Agent, Forecast/Reporting Agent, Cancellation Workflow Preparer.

## Deterministic services

Bank/accounting/AP import, invoice parser, vendor/entity matcher, contract store, renewal scheduler, SSO/app usage connectors, seat calculator, overlap taxonomy, savings ledger, approval workflow, notification, audit, protected cancellation/purchase gate.

## Workflow

Discover spend/apps → normalize vendor/product → link contract/owner/usage → compute utilization/renewal/risk → identify waste/overlap → recommend → owner/policy approve → future cancellation/downgrade action through scoped connector → verify bill stops/seat removed → savings evidence.

## Integrations / API / data

Accounting/AP, corporate card/bank data, SSO/identity, SaaS admin APIs, HRIS/offboarding, email for invoices/contracts, procurement, contract management, vendor risk, helpdesk. APIs for inventory, spend, seats, renewals, recommendations, savings evidence.

Data moat: normalized vendor/product catalog, pricing/contract benchmark history, overlap graph, utilization patterns and verified savings outcomes, with customer financial data isolated.

## Monetization / distribution

Subscription by spend/company/seats/apps, verified-savings share, enterprise, managed service, API/OEM. Distribution via finance/procurement/IT ecosystems, accounting/SSO partners, MSPs/consultants.

## Security/privacy/legal

Financial/contracts/employee usage sensitive. Minimize employee-level visibility, role-based access, secret refs, no hidden surveillance, contract confidentiality, protected purchase/cancel actions, no misrepresentation in negotiations.

## Failure/edge cases

Vendor descriptor mismatch, annual prepaid spend, shared account, inactive but critical seat, renewal notice already passed, bundled products, contract amendments, departmental politics, license needed for legal hold, billing lag, false savings attribution. Use confidence, owner/app-owner review, verify downstream invoices before claiming savings.

## Scale/platform/overlap

Micro → SMB spend visibility → enterprise procurement stack → SaaS operations/optimization platform. Adjacent #23 contract obligations, #24 vendor risk, #32 procurement, #58 offboarding, #85 revenue leakage analog but buyer-side.
