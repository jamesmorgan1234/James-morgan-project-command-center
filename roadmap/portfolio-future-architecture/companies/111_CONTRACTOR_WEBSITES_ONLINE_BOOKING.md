## Business identity

Purpose: Provide independent trades and local service businesses with fast, trustworthy, accessible websites and practical owner-controlled online inquiry/appointment workflows at clear fixed scope.

Core problem: Many local contractors have outdated mobile sites, incomplete service-area information, weak contact forms, and disconnected inquiry calendars; custom agency delivery is often slow and expensive.

Value proposition: Trade-specialized production templates, verified mobile UX, real lead capture delivery, clear ownership/portability and isolated customer-owned domains and contact accounts.

Ideal first customer: Solo tradespeople and small service companies who want a professional website and reliable inquiry intake without owning a complex marketing stack.

Monetization model hypothesis: One-time template build and configuration service, optional recurring update/maintenance package, optional approved integrations, no paid hosting/provider dependency mandatory to prepare an initial site.

Non-goal and protected boundary: Company designs/builds websites and lead-capture infrastructure; RevoCapture remains independent and owns recovery/follow-up products. No default CRM/SMS campaigns, no invented reviews or search ranking guarantees, no company-wide shared client contact identity.
