- **Abuse / misuse:** unauthorized redistribution, credential sharing, prohibited enrichment, re-identification, source circumvention, bulk extraction beyond entitlement, poisoned uploads and attempts to bypass source-use restrictions.
- **Observability:** source freshness, ingest lag, schema drift, record volume, duplicate/conflict rate, entity-resolution quality, API/feed latency, correction rate, entitlement/meter anomalies, source cost and license status.
- **Incident response:** quarantine bad source, stop publishing affected datasets, mark stale/unknown, rollback/rebuild point-in-time snapshots, revoke exports/feeds where contractually required, notify authorized customers and preserve correction evidence.
- **Testing / Brain evaluation:** source-contract checks, schema/data-quality, entity-resolution, dedupe, lineage, freshness, deterministic transforms, regression, privacy/rights, malicious-input and benchmark/index reproducibility tests; Brain evaluated for source conflict reasoning, provenance fidelity and no invented facts.

- **Performance / cost:** ingest throughput, query/feed latency, storage/history growth, source/API cost, transform cost, egress, cache/search cost and cost per record/query/feed/customer.
- **Financial metrics:** if commercialized later, MRR/ARR, gross/contribution margin, source/license cost, cost per dataset/record/query, support cost, churn/retention and expansion; test/sample usage is never revenue.
- **Internationalization / accessibility / mobile / voice / multimodal:** country/source-language packs, region/residency handling and locale-aware human-facing metadata; accessible dashboards/docs; mobile primarily alerts/status; voice optional; multimodal source products require modality-specific provenance and rights.
- **Training / community / content:** data dictionaries, source notes, lineage documentation, integration guides, analyst/developer training, quality methodology and a governed data-user community without source/customer leakage.
- **Experimentation / continuous improvement:** shadow new sources/transforms/entity models and compare against historical snapshots; no silent source-rights expansion or customer-visible schema break. Verified corrections/incidents become regression tests.
- **Kill / safe mode:** per source/dataset/feed/schema/transform/customer disable, stale/frozen snapshot mode, read-only historical access, stop export, revoke compromised credentials and roll back candidate pipelines.
- **Exit / portability:** documented schemas, point-in-time exports, provenance/history, customer configuration/entitlements, correction records and migration windows; revoke credentials and delete/retain according to law/contracts.
- **Reuse / internalization:** connector, schema, lineage, quality, entity resolution, CDC, snapshotting, alerts, metering, entitlement and observability are shared candidates only after proof. Repeated commercial data/provider dependencies are assessed for lawful free/open/public/internal alternatives without authorizing acquisition.
- **Blast radius:** shared source connectors, entity-resolution logic, schema contracts and lineage services can corrupt many datasets; use source/dataset scoping, candidate versions, canaries, quarantines, reconciliation and forkability.
- **Version / compatibility:** source terms, schemas, transforms, entity models, benchmarks/indices, APIs/feeds and Brain/agent versions are explicit and historical snapshots remain reproducible.
