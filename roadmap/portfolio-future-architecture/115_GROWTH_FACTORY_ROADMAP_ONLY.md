# 115 — Growth Factory — ROADMAP ONLY

**Status:** FUTURE ROADMAP ONLY — NO MARKETING/OUTREACH AUTHORIZED

## Purpose
Map reusable growth/distribution infrastructure future companies may inherit, without creating accounts, domains, campaigns, posts, outreach, ads or customer acquisition now.

## Capability families
- landing-page/content schema templates;
- SEO technical/content metadata;
- organic content planning;
- app/marketplace listing metadata;
- referral/affiliate program contracts;
- attribution/event taxonomy;
- trial/onboarding conversion metrics;
- lifecycle-message templates tied to consent;
- product-led growth experiments;
- experiment registry and holdouts;
- campaign/content asset provenance;
- partner/reseller metadata;
- growth analytics and unit economics;
- brand/company identity separation;
- localization/accessibility;
- abuse/spam/consent safeguards.

## Strict boundaries
No unsolicited outreach, account creation, public posting, paid advertising, domain purchase, affiliate enrollment, email/SMS campaign, scraping of private data, or live opportunity hunting is authorized by this architecture work.

## Data model
Future growth record should bind company/product/customer-segment, channel, asset/version, consent basis, audience rule, experiment/version, spend budget, source/attribution, metrics, time window and verified outcome. Forecast/impression/click/lead/customer/revenue states remain distinct.

## Cross-company isolation
Brands, domains, social identities, mailing lists, customer records, pixels/analytics and ad accounts are never shared merely because the companies share an owner.

## Shared deterministic services
UTM/attribution IDs; experiment assignment; funnel event definitions; consent checks; send/publish preflight; rate limits; suppression/opt-out; content versioning; analytics calculations; budget ceilings.

## Brain/Agent future roles
Research/SEO planner, content planner, experiment analyst, lifecycle planner and attribution analyst may recommend. Public publishing, outbound communication and spend remain protected actions.

## DESIGN NOW
Company brand/account identity, attribution/event contracts, consent/preference integration, content/asset provenance and protected publish/send/spend action classes.

## BUILD NOW
No marketing execution feature. Ensure shared Command Center analytics, company registry, communications and policy contracts can later attach brand/channel/attribution metadata without redesign.

## BUILD BEFORE FIRST COMPANY
Only when launch is separately authorized: company web/domain identity, analytics, consent, product messaging, lifecycle communications and experiment registry.

## Acceptance
A dummy company can define a hypothetical funnel/experiment/content asset entirely offline without generating a live campaign, public post, customer contact or spend.
