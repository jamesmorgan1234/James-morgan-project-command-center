Tests now supply explicit synthetic evidence for positive cases; no fixture is actual revenue. Missing, test, mismatched or estimated evidence cannot become confirmed money. Confirmed causal attribution additionally needs declared direct-provider evidence; otherwise attribution remains estimated/unknown, even when the outcome itself is confirmed.

SQL adds truthful nullable metric states, finite-number/window/inference constraints and canonical brand/account guards. A private immutable verified-outcome reference table is writable only by trusted server ingestion; ordinary clients cannot mint metrics/attributions/outcome attestations. Revenue insert trigger requires matching independent non-test outcome. Outcome bridging must independently validate provider/financial evidence under its originating authorization boundary; social permissions do not grant finance reads. No automatic copying of private finance data. Causal basis/assumptions are stored separately; views preserve those fields and select observation chronology.
