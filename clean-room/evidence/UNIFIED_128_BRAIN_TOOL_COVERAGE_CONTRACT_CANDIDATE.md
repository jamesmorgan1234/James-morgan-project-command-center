## Capability-complete admission rules

For **each Brain and each logical role**, the future tool-admission manifest MUST map:

1. Canonical owner, project, company, Brain/version and specialist truth owner.
2. Exact source-defined task/capability and source version/hash; no generic compressed substitute.
3. Candidate tool/tool-family plus whether a real, existing $0 provider can meet the task, or whether local/offline implementation is needed.
4. Input/output schemas, required integrations, maximum resource/cost ceiling, prerequisites, task lifecycle, timeouts and retries.
5. Owner/project/account/data scopes (exact allowlist) and forbidden credential substitution.
6. Risk class; which internal reversible operations can proceed under a valid bounded task, and which actions require fresh action-specific owner approval.
7. Verification method (real tool invocation, deterministic oracle, provider readback, negative-case rejection, and evidence receipt).
8. Graceful degradation and alternatives when a tool is absent, offline, outdated or insufficient; never silently substitute a paid or unauthorized provider.
9. Task-specific audit, idempotency/duplicate-effect protection, stop/pause/freeze, checkpoints and rollback.
10. Explicit coverage status: AVAILABLE_AND_VERIFIED, IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED, NEEDED_NOT_IMPLEMENTED, NOT_APPLICABLE_WITH_EVIDENCE, or BLOCKED.

**No arbitrary minimal-tool cap:** evaluate the complete role/capability tree, even when it requires multiple cooperating tools. **No blanket all-tools authorization:** grant exactly the validated capability with its separate account/project and per-action risk boundary.
